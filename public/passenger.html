Engineering

Why Passenger installers choose APEAX.

Self-Healing Hardcoat

Aliphatic polyurethane top layer recovers from swirl marks and light scratches with residual heat. No additional curing step in the install.

UV-Stabilised PET Core

Optical-grade PET base with UV-stabilised chemistry. 99% harmful ray rejection across the full warranty window.

Hydrophobic Surface

Water sheet-off reduces mineral staining, makes wash cycles faster, and keeps the customer's car presentable between details.

Clear Acrylic Adhesive

Clean removal inside the warranty window. Low-residue. Re-installer friendly if the customer wants a refresh.
